Design Philosophy

My design philosophy focuses on simplicity, functionality, and professionalism. The portfolio uses a clean dark blue color scheme to create a modern and organized appearance while maintaining readability. Each section is arranged clearly, allowing visitors to easily explore personal information, projects, and contact details. The project showcase emphasizes completed works through a structured layout, highlighting both technical skills and creativity. By combining a professional visual style with user-friendly navigation, the design provides an engaging experience that effectively presents content in a clear and organized manner.
